## API / MCP access

Personal Access Tokens (PATs) are available on all plans. Rate limits:

| Plan | Requests / min | Concurrent renders |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 20 | 1 |
| Starter/Creator | 60 | 3 |
| Pro | 200 | 10 |
| Agency | unlimited | negotiated |

MCP server install: `npx viewmagnet-mcp install`. Requires a PAT from https://viewmagnet.io/dashboard/api-tokens.

## FAQ

**Is there a free trial?** Yes — all paid plans include a 30-day free trial, no credit card required to start.

**Can I cancel anytime?** Yes. Cancel before the trial ends and you will not be charged.

**Is the Agency plan right for me?** The Agency plan suits multi-channel managers, MCN operators, and agencies who manage content for multiple clients. It includes white-label reports and team collaboration seats.

**Do you offer annual billing?** Contact sales@viewmagnet.io for annual pricing (typically 20% off).
